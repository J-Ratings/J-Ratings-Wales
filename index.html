Here you’ll find WCU ratings calculated with a live batch system. Instead of fixing ratings for six months and recalculating later, live batches update continuously after each game. I created this site mainly for players who play a large number of games each season, as they are the ones most affected by rating swings under the 6-month batch system.

Under the current WCU system, all results are tied to a player's rating from the start of the batch. For most players this isn’t an issue, but for those who play many games it can have a bigger impact. Their results only show once the next list is published, which can cause ratings to overshoot or undershoot, and in extreme cases lead to a yo-yo effect where ratings struggle to stabilise. Live batches aim to reduce this, giving active players a steadier and more realistic picture of their playing strength.

The official January 2022 WCU list was taken as the starting point, with all calculations applied from there.

The official ratings remain those published on the WCU grading site. That site does all the work of collecting results, maintaining records, and producing the official rating lists. J-Ratings simply copies those published results and applies a different calculation method. From time to time I run some code to refresh the numbers—there’s no separate database or ongoing manual work involved here.
