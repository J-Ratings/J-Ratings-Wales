Here you’ll find unofficial WCU ratings calculated with a live batch system, which updates continuously after each game rather than waiting for the 6-month update.

I built this mainly for players who play a large number of games each season, as they are most affected when results are tied to their initial ratings from the start of a batch. For them, the official system can produce overshoots, undershoots, and even a yo-yo effect where ratings struggle to stabilise, while live batches give a steadier and more realistic picture of current strength.

A player's WCU rating before their first game will be taken as the starting point for their J-Ratings, with all live calculations applied from there.

The official ratings are those published on the WCU grading site. That site does all the work of collecting results, maintaining records, and producing the official rating lists. J-Ratings simply copies those published results and applies a different calculation method. From time to time I run some code to refresh the numbers. There’s no separate database or ongoing manual work involved here.
